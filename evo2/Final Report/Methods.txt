For our methodology, we adopt a zero-shot prediction approach using a pretrained protein language model. This means we do not fine-tune the model on mutation-specific data, but directly use it to evaluate mutation impact.

The evaluation is based on a given benchmark dataset of labeled variants, where each mutation is annotated as either pathogenic or benign.

We calculate scores such as log-likelihood ratio (LLR) or delta_score, which reflect how the model perceives the sequence likelihood before and after mutation. These scores are used to rank the variants.

Finally, we compute the Area Under the Curve (AUC) to assess how well the model distinguishes pathogenic from benign variants. A higher AUC indicates better discrimination capability.
